Privacy Policy

Rotary & Rotaract Youth Festival 2026 – Naya Bharat 2047

This Privacy Policy explains how information submitted through the official website and registration system of the Rotary & Rotaract Youth Festival 2026 (“Event”, “we”, “us” or “our”) may be collected, used, stored and protected.

By using the Event website or registering for the Event, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the service or registration process used, we may collect information including:

Participant Information

  • First Name
  • Last Name
  • Mobile Number
  • Email ID
  • Date of Birth
  • College/organization and city, when voluntarily provided
  • Selected Event date
  • Number of participants
  • Registration/session details

Payment Information

Payments may be processed through a third-party payment gateway.

We may receive transaction-related information such as:

  • Payment status;
  • Transaction/reference number;
  • Payment gateway order ID;
  • Payment ID;
  • Amount paid;
  • Date and time of transaction.

We do not intend to collect or store complete card numbers, CVV numbers, UPI PINs, passwords or other sensitive payment credentials that are directly processed by the payment gateway.

2. How We Use Information

Information collected may be used for:

  • Processing Event registrations;
  • Generating Registration IDs;
  • Confirming ticket bookings;
  • Processing and verifying payments;
  • Managing Event entry;
  • Communicating Event-related information;
  • Sending registration confirmations;
  • Providing support;
  • Managing food coupon eligibility where applicable;
  • Preventing duplicate or fraudulent registrations;
  • Managing Event capacity;
  • Maintaining Event records;
  • Handling refund or payment queries;
  • Improving Event administration;
  • Complying with legal or regulatory requirements.

3. Communication

We may contact registered participants using the information provided during registration for legitimate Event-related purposes, including:

  • Registration confirmation;
  • Payment confirmation;
  • Event reminders;
  • Venue information;
  • Schedule changes;
  • Important safety instructions;
  • Cancellation/postponement information;
  • Support and registration-related communication.

4. Information Relating to Children and Minors

The Event may be attended by participants below 18 years of age.

For minors, the person completing the registration should have the appropriate authority and consent to provide the participant's information.

For participants below 16 years of age, parent/legal guardian consent may be required as part of the registration process.

Parents/legal guardians should ensure that information submitted for minors is accurate.

5. Payment Service Providers

Payment transactions may be processed through third-party payment service providers.

Such providers may process payment-related information in accordance with their own privacy policies and terms.

The organisers may receive transaction confirmation and limited payment-related information necessary to reconcile and administer Event registrations.

6. Service Providers

We may use authorised technology/service providers for purposes such as:

  • Website hosting;
  • Registration management;
  • Database management;
  • Payment processing;
  • Communication;
  • Security;
  • Analytics;
  • Technical support.

Such providers may process information only to the extent necessary for providing the relevant service, subject to applicable arrangements and requirements.

7. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures may be used to protect information from unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

However, no internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Participants should not share passwords, OTPs, UPI PINs, card PINs or other confidential payment credentials with Event representatives.

8. Data Retention

Participant and transaction information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for:

  • Event administration;
  • Accounting and financial reconciliation;
  • Customer support;
  • Legal and regulatory requirements;
  • Dispute resolution;
  • Record-keeping;
  • Fraud prevention.

After the information is no longer required, it may be deleted, anonymised or securely archived, subject to applicable requirements.

9. Sharing of Information

Personal information will not ordinarily be sold or rented to third parties.

Information may be shared where reasonably necessary with:

  • Payment service providers;
  • Technology/service providers;
  • Event partners or authorised personnel;
  • Venue/security personnel where necessary for Event administration or safety;
  • Government or regulatory authorities where legally required;
  • Professional advisers where reasonably necessary.

10. Photography and Videography

The Event may be photographed, filmed or recorded.

Images or recordings captured at the Event may be used for Event documentation, reporting, publicity, social media, websites, presentations and promotional activities.

Participants who have concerns regarding the use of their identifiable image may contact the organisers.

11. Cookies and Technical Information

The website may use cookies, analytics tools or similar technologies for purposes such as:

  • Website functionality;
  • Security;
  • Performance monitoring;
  • Understanding website usage;
  • Improving user experience.

Users may be able to control cookies through their browser settings, although disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

12. Third-Party Websites

The Event website may contain links or references to third-party websites, services or platforms.

The organisers are not responsible for the privacy practices, security or content of third-party websites.

Users should review the privacy policies of third-party services before submitting information to them.

13. User Rights and Requests

Subject to applicable law, an individual may contact the organisers regarding:

  • Access to information submitted;
  • Correction of inaccurate information;
  • Questions about use of personal information;
  • Other privacy-related concerns.

Requests may be subject to reasonable verification before action is taken.

14. Changes to this Privacy Policy

The organisers may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Any updated version may be published on the Event website with a revised “Last Updated” date.
