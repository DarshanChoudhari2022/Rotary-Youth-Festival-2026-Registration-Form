Refund & Cancellation Policy

Rotary & Rotaract Youth Festival 2026 – Naya Bharat 2047

General policy

Bookings are final and non-refundable, subject to this policy and applicable law. Before paying, verify the visit date, quantity, names, dates of birth, mobile numbers, email addresses and other booking details.

Non-refundable tickets

Confirmed tickets are generally not refunded for changes in personal or travel plans, inability to attend, late arrival, non-attendance, incorrect date or quantity, or missing information.

Non-transferability

Tickets are non-transferable unless the organisers expressly approve a change. Sale, resale and commercial transfer require written approval.

Duplicate payments

Contact support with the registration ID, payment reference, transaction date, amount and proof. The organisers may verify the transaction and process any eligible refund.

Payment deducted without confirmation

Contact support with the transaction details. The organisers may verify payment before confirming the booking, taking corrective action or initiating an eligible refund.

Event cancellation

The organisers will communicate applicable refunds or alternative arrangements. Approved refunds may be processed through the original payment method or another appropriate method.

Postponement

Registrations may remain valid for a rescheduled event at the organisers’ discretion. The organisers will communicate the procedure.

Programme changes

Changes to speakers, activities, exhibitors, performances or timings do not automatically qualify for a refund.

Processing times

The organisers will initiate an approved refund within a reasonable period. Credit times depend on the gateway, bank and payment method.

Contact and final provision

For refunds, contact Dr. Santosh More, District Co-Director, on 9011023332. Include the registration ID and payment reference. Read this policy with the Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy and Payment Terms. The organisers may review individual cases where permitted by applicable law.
