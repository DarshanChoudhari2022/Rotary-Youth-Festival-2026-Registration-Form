1. About the Event

The Rotary & Rotaract Youth Festival 2026 – Naya Bharat 2047 is a youth-focused festival bringing together students, young people, educational institutions, industry representatives, innovators, entrepreneurs, professionals and the wider community.

The Event will feature educational, cultural, technological, career, entrepreneurial and experiential activities.

The Event may include, among other activities:

  • Art and Craft
  • Music and Drama
  • Career and Job Fair
  • Startup and Entrepreneurship initiatives
  • Indian Food Festival
  • Technology and Innovation showcases
  • AI and emerging technologies
  • DRDO, NDRF and CRPF-related showcases
  • Agro-tech
  • 3D Printing
  • Aircraft and Aviation-related displays
  • Drone and technology demonstrations
  • Medical and Pharmaceutical showcases
  • Textile and industry-related experiences
  • Other educational, cultural and youth-oriented activities
