Rotary & Rotaract Youth Festival 2026 – Naya Bharat 2047

Ticket price

₹200 per participant per day. Choose 21 November or 22 November 2026 at ₹200 per participant, or both days at ₹400 per participant.

Food coupons

Participants below 16 are eligible for the applicable food coupon. Participants aged 16 and above receive no coupon under the standard ticket arrangement. The price is the same for all ages. Date of birth may be used to verify eligibility.

Booking limits and totals

Maximum ten participants per booking. One, five and ten participants cost ₹200, ₹1,000 and ₹2,000 respectively for one day, or ₹400, ₹2,000 and ₹4,000 for both days.

Payment methods

The authorised gateway may offer UPI, debit and credit cards, net banking and other enabled payment methods.

Confirmation

Registration is confirmed only after payment verification. Retain the confirmation for entry. It may include the registration ID, amount, dates, participants and transaction reference.

Pending, failed or deducted payments

Check your registration or payment status or contact support before trying again. If money was deducted without confirmation, provide the payment reference. The organisers may verify the payment before confirming the registration or processing an eligible refund.

Gateway and additional charges

The authorised gateway processes payment information under its own terms and privacy policy. Any separate charges will be shown before payment where technically applicable.

Refunds and fraud

See the Refund & Cancellation Policy. Registrations may be suspended or cancelled for suspected fraud, manipulation, duplicate or unauthorised transactions, false information or misuse.
