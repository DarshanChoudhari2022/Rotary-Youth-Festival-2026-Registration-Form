Authority and consent

For a participant below 18, the person registering confirms they are the participant’s parent, legal guardian or authorised person and have the necessary consent to provide their details and register them.

Consent and confirmation

The parent or guardian confirms the information is accurate, that they have authority to provide the minor’s name, mobile number, email address and date of birth, and that they authorise participation. Activities may include educational, cultural, technological, career, exhibition and interactive experiences.

Rules and communication

The minor must follow event rules and safety instructions. The event may be photographed or recorded under the Privacy Policy. The organisers may use the supplied contact details for event communications. Inform organisers of information reasonably needed for safe participation.

Policies, programme and supervision

The parent or guardian acknowledges the Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy, Refund & Cancellation Policy and Entry Policy, and that the programme may change. Parents and guardians are responsible for supervision as applicable. Organisers take reasonable safety measures and cannot assume responsibility for circumstances beyond their reasonable control.

Declaration

By selecting the consent checkbox, the parent, guardian or authorised person confirms that they have read this statement and consent to the minor’s participation.
