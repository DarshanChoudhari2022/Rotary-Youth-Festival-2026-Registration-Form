Event Participation & Entry Policy

Rotary & Rotaract Youth Festival 2026 – Naya Bharat 2047

Registration and verification

Ticketed areas require valid registration. Be prepared to show a confirmation, ticket or QR code, registration ID or identification where required. Staff may request additional verification if details differ.

Minors

Participants below 18 may be subject to additional entry or supervision requirements. Parents and guardians are responsible for the minors under their supervision. Food coupon eligibility for participants below 16 is based on the submitted date of birth.

Security and restricted areas

Cooperate with reasonable security checks and venue instructions. Restricted, staff, backstage and technical areas are for authorised personnel only. Refusal to comply may result in denial of entry.

Conduct and removals

Violence, threats, harassment, damage, disruption, trespass, ignoring safety instructions and unlawful activity are prohibited. The organisers may remove participants for misconduct or safety risks. Removal generally does not qualify for a refund.

Food and personal belongings

See the Food Coupon Policy for eligibility, availability and redemption. The organisers are not responsible for lost, stolen or damaged belongings except where required by law.

Emergencies

Notify event staff immediately and follow instructions from staff, venue authorities and emergency personnel.

Photography and programme changes

The event may be recorded for documentation and promotion. Attendees may appear in general photographs or recordings. Programme details may change. The organisers will communicate significant changes where reasonably possible.

Compliance

Participants must comply with this policy, the Terms & Conditions, venue rules, safety instructions and applicable law.
