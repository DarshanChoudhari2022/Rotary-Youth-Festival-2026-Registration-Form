Food Coupon Policy

Rotary & Rotaract Youth Festival 2026 – Naya Bharat 2047

Eligibility and price

Participants below 16 are eligible for the applicable food coupon. Participants aged 16 and above are ineligible under the standard ticket arrangement. The fee remains ₹200 per participant per day.

Age verification

The organisers may use date of birth and request verification. Incorrect or deliberately misleading age details may lead to loss of coupon eligibility.

Distribution and redemption

Coupons may be issued at registration, at a designated counter or through another process communicated at the event. Redeem at designated outlets. Items, quantities, timings and availability may be limited.

Cash, transfer and replacement

Coupons generally cannot be exchanged for cash or transferred. Unused coupons are ordinarily non-refundable. Lost, damaged or misused coupons may not be replaced.

Dietary requirements

Verify ingredients with the food provider if you have allergies, dietary restrictions or special requirements. The organisers cannot guarantee that all food options are suitable.

Changes and decisions

Food arrangements may change for availability, safety, venue or operational reasons. The organisers will make reasonable efforts to communicate significant changes. Their eligibility, distribution and redemption decisions are subject to applicable law.
